## Introducing our new Hero Tech Course: Berlin Edition!

The Hero Tech Course is a developer training program that brings the educational philosophy and curriculum of Woowa Tech Course (one of Korea's most respected programming education programs) to Europe, with the goal of nurturing developers with global competitiveness.

Woowa Tech Course, operated by Woowa Brothers (the company behind Korea's leading delivery platform, "Baemin"), has been running since 2019 and is widely recognized in Korea for producing job-ready junior developers. The program focuses on project-based learning, pair programming, code reviews, and self-directed learning, simulating real-world development environments. To date, it has trained and launched the careers of hundreds of junior developers in Korea's tech industry.

Over the past seven years, many graduates have joined top tech companies in Korea, and in 2024, several joined Delivery Hero entities in Berlin and Dubai, demonstrating that the program prepares developers to succeed in the global market.

Now, for the first time, this proven educational model is expanding globally, starting with its launch at Delivery Hero's headquarters in Berlin.

## Learning Objectives & Curriculum

The Hero Tech Course is a two-month program focused on Kotlin-based backend development and project-based learning. It helps developers build both fundamental programming skills and essential soft skills.

**Training Field**: Server/Backend Development

**What you'll experience**

- Backend web development using Kotlin and the Spring Framework
- Hands-on learning of essential backend skills including object-oriented programming and Test-Driven Development (TDD)
- Clean code practices through pair programming and code reviews

**You'll have the opportunity to**

- Work on real-world projects to gain practical experience
- Develop problem-solving skills and a self-directed learning approach
- Enhance your collaboration and communication skills in a global environment

## After Completion of the Course

- Participants who successfully complete the course will have the opportunity to join a 3-month internship at Delivery Hero from September to December 2025.
- Internship participants will be considered for full-time engineering positions at Delivery Hero in 2026 based on performance and role.

## We're Looking For Those Who

- Want to grow as a developer on a global stage and collaborate in a diverse environment
- Are motivated, committed, and determined to complete required projects and challenges
- Have a passion for continuous and independent learning, beyond simply acquiring knowledge
- Enjoy learning and growing together through code reviews, pair programming, and team projects

## Eligibility

- Relevant educational background (major in Computer Science, IT, etc.) or a graduate of a bootcamp/technical upskilling program
- Self-taught coders with at least 6 months of experience who are career-transitioning are also welcome to apply
- Demonstrated basic programming skills (ability to write programs of 300–500 lines)
- Business fluency in English
- Ability to commute daily to Delivery Hero's Berlin office during the training period
- Available for an in-house internship from Sept–Dec 2025 (preferred) or ready to start a full-time role in early 2026
- Legally eligible to live and work in Germany (including those with EU passports)
